## 1. Product Summary

Build a contemporary landing page and enquiry workflow for an independent, remote creative studio that produces product listing images and visual content for e-commerce sellers.

The studio's primary specialization is Amazon. Other platforms—including eBay, Allegro, Etsy, Shopify, online stores, and other marketplaces—are supported extensions rather than the primary hero message.

The service is research-led: the studio analyzes the product, category, competitors, reviews, and buyer questions before creating visual content. The deliverable is not merely photo processing or decorative imagery; it is visual content intended to make a product easier to understand and easier to choose.

The first version should prioritize:

- A clear, high-quality landing page.
- A real portfolio presentation area with placeholders until assets are supplied.
- An enquiry form that supports multiple SKUs/products.
- Product image uploads.
- A preliminary price calculation, if approved for V1.
- Organized submission handling.
- Owner notifications and a client confirmation email.

The first version does not need to include a full customer account system, online card payments, a customer portal, or automated invoicing.

## 2. Goals and Success Criteria

### 2.1 Business goals

1. Explain the service and Amazon focus immediately.
2. Show that the work is based on product understanding and customer research.
3. Convert qualified visitors into structured project enquiries.
4. Collect enough product, SKU, platform, and file information for a personal scope review.
5. Communicate approved pricing without creating a misleading checkout or automatic quote.
6. Establish trust through process clarity, real portfolio work, and professional legal placeholders.

### 2.2 User goals

A prospective client should be able to:

1. Understand what the studio creates.
2. Determine whether the service fits their product and sales platform.
3. See the process, expected timing, pricing rules, revisions, delivery, and payment terms.
4. Submit one or more products/SKUs with relevant information and files.
5. Receive confirmation that the request was received.
6. Know that the final scope and price will be personally confirmed.

### 2.3 Product success criteria

The implementation is successful when:

- The hero section communicates product listing visuals, customer-focused thinking, and Amazon relevance without unsupported claims.
- The user can navigate from understanding the service to starting a project without confusion.
- The form captures all required contact and product data.
- Multiple SKUs can be submitted in one enquiry.
- The pricing logic enforces the minimum of 3 images per product and clearly communicates the €200 order minimum.
- Uploads are associated with the correct product/SKU.
- The owner receives structured enquiry data and notifications.
- The client receives a confirmation email containing a request ID and summary.
- No fictional portfolio entries, testimonials, performance figures, or legal/company data appear in production.

## 6. Service Model and Delivery Rules

The studio's working process is:

1. Receive white-background product photos, product information, and an existing listing URL if available.
2. Analyze the product's characteristics, features, benefits, and use cases.
3. Research competitors on Amazon and other relevant platforms.
4. Review positive and negative customer reviews to identify recurring questions, problems, and valued benefits.
5. Identify additional insights only when the client's product can substantiate the relevant claim.
6. Define a product-specific visual strategy; do not force every product into one universal sequence.
7. Create visual content.
8. Review the existing product description and, when needed, provide improved SEO-optimized text in a separate editable document.
9. Organize final files by product and SKU.

Possible visual deliverables include:

- Improved product images.
- AI-generated lifestyle images.
- Product-in-use images.
- Infographics.
- Feature visualizations.
- Benefit-focused images.
- Images answering common buyer questions.

Amazon main-image requirements differ from additional-image requirements. The implementation and marketing copy must not suggest that props, decorative elements, or AI scenes can always be used in the main image. The designer/owner must validate platform/category rules before publishing relevant claims.

### 6.1 Description-review bonus

The free bonus is a review of an existing product description. When it is insufficiently informative, poorly structured, or missing relevant search queries, the studio may provide an improved SEO-optimized version.

This is text optimization, not a guarantee of search position or ranking.

## 7. Approved Pricing and Commercial Rules

| Offer | Rule | Required UI treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Individual images | €10 per image; minimum 3 images per product (€30 minimum for that product) | Let the client select quantity from 3 onward |
| 9-image set | €65 | Visually highlight this option |
| Overall order minimum | €200 for the entire order | Multiple SKUs may be combined; the €65 set alone does not qualify |
| Description-review bonus | Free review and improved SEO-optimized text when needed | Describe as a separate editable document |
| Rush work | +30% to order value, only if capacity exists and after individual confirmation | Do not present as automatically available |

### 7.1 Pricing restrictions

- Do not display a crossed-out comparison price until approved.
- A previously discussed €80 comparison price is not approved.
- Nine individual images would otherwise equal €90, but the only currently approved 9-image set price is €65.
- The €200 minimum applies to the complete order, not each SKU.
- The calculator is a preliminary estimate only, not an automatic quote, invoice, or checkout.
- Final scope, availability, and price require personal confirmation.

### 7.2 Timing, payment, revisions, and delivery

**Timing**

- Normal delivery target: up to 5 working days.
- Exact timing depends on scope and current capacity and is confirmed before work starts.
- Earlier delivery may happen but must not be promised.

**Payment**

- 50% prepayment.
- Remaining 50% after result approval and before original files are delivered.
- An official payment document is issued with company details.
- EU clients need a way to provide company and VAT/NIP data.
- Tax, invoice, proforma, and country-specific rules require final accounting/legal guidance; do not invent them.

**Revisions**

- Two rounds of revisions are included.
- One revision round applies to the whole order.
- The client should consolidate feedback into one list.
- Normal revisions may include text, colors, individual elements, and composition.
- A complete change of approved concept is not automatically a normal revision.
- Third and later rounds are charged separately based on agreed scope.

**Preview and delivery**

- Pre-payment completion: only watermarked previews are shared.
- Original files without watermark are delivered after full payment.
- Preferred delivery method: Google Drive.
- Proposed structure:

```text
Order ID / Product SKU / Images / SEO Description
```

- EAN may be used in product/file organization when applicable.

**Retention**

- Files remain on Google Drive for one month after order completion.
- Longer retention is individually agreed and charged separately.

## 9. Preliminary Calculator — Candidate V1

The calculator is a candidate V1 feature and remains subject to owner approval.

### 9.1 Calculation rules

For each SKU:

1. Read the selected image count.
2. Reject or explain counts below 3.
3. If the count is exactly 9, use the approved set price of €65.
4. Otherwise, use `€10 × selected image count` unless the owner approves another rule for quantities above 9.
5. Sum all SKU subtotals.
6. If the total is below €200, show the order-minimum rule clearly.
7. If rush work is selected, show a provisional `+30%` calculation while clearly stating that availability and final price require confirmation.

### 9.2 Pricing ambiguity to resolve before implementation

The brief explicitly approves a €65 price for exactly 9 images, but it does not fully specify the treatment of 10+ images. The owner must confirm one of the following before the calculator is treated as final:

- Per-image pricing for every quantity except exactly 9.
- Additional package tiers.
- A custom/manual quote above 9 images.

Until resolved, use a clearly marked implementation configuration rather than hard-coding an irreversible commercial decision.

### 9.3 Calculator copy

The UI must state:

> This is a preliminary estimate, not an automatic offer or invoice. We will review your request and confirm the final scope, availability, and price personally.

The UI must not present a below-€200 enquiry as ready for checkout.

## 10. Submission and Notification Workflow

### 10.1 Desired workflow

1. Client submits the form.
2. The website assigns a unique enquiry/order number.
3. Files and structured data are stored in organized storage, preferably Google Drive.
4. Owner receives an email notification.
5. Owner receives a Telegram notification.
6. Client receives a confirmation email.
7. Owner contacts the client personally.
8. Scope and final price are agreed.
9. Owner issues the payment document.
10. Work begins after receipt of the 50% prepayment.

Online card payment is not approved and is not required by default.

### 10.2 Storage requirements

Preferred storage structure:

<Request ID> /

    Images/
    SEO Description/
