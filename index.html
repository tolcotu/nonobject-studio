A few things
worth knowing.

How long does a project take?+

Normal delivery target is up to 5 working days. Exact timing depends on your scope and current capacity and is confirmed before work begins. Rush work is only available by agreement, with a provisional 30% surcharge.

What do you need from me?+

Clear white-background product photos, accurate product information, your SKU and an existing listing URL if available. Include dimensions, materials, benefits you can substantiate and any brand guidelines. Upload JPG, PNG or WebP images with your enquiry.

Can I combine different products?+

Yes. Multiple SKUs can be combined in one enquiry. The €200 minimum applies to the whole order, with a minimum of 3 images per product. The highlighted 9-image set costs €65 per product, so one set alone does not meet the order minimum.

How do payment and revisions work?+

Payment is split 50% before work starts and 50% after approval, before original files are delivered. An official payment document includes company details. Two revision rounds are included for the whole order; send each round as one consolidated list. A change of approved concept and additional rounds are quoted separately.

Is this only for Amazon?+

Amazon is our main focus. We also create visuals for eBay, Allegro, Etsy, Shopify and other online stores. Main-image rules differ from additional-image rules; each platform and category’s requirements are checked for the agreed scope.

How will I receive my files?+

You review watermarked previews first. After full payment, original files are delivered, preferably through Google Drive, organized by order and SKU. Completed files remain available for one month. Longer retention is agreed and charged separately.

Is the estimate my final price?+

No. The calculator is a preliminary estimate, not an automatic offer, invoice or checkout. We review your materials and confirm the final scope, availability and price personally. Quantities above 9 images need a custom quote.
