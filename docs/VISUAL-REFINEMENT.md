# NONOBJECT editorial refinement

## Current direction: portfolio-led studio

Following the user’s feedback that the previous pass still felt AI-generated, the opening now uses supplied baking-dish imagery: the isolated product paired with the completed lifestyle visual. The generated orange bottle is no longer used on the page. A compact NONOBJECT masthead replaces the giant opening wordmark, and a sans-serif / italic serif contrast gives the retained slogan its hierarchy. Project titles are descriptive product names. The research section uses a muted light surface rather than a large dark slab, and the oversized orange footer is removed. Movement is limited to a small difference in travel between the product and finished visual, with a static reduced-motion composition. This direction supersedes the earlier campaign-image decisions below.

## Previous iteration (superseded)

The user asked for stronger main imagery, less visual noise, a clearer section sequence and a more premium experience. This revision uses the existing paper, ink and orange palette and typographic conventions. The original slogan, offer and short enquiry requirements remain intact.

## Decisions

- Journey: hero and offer → selected work → one research example → pricing → practical answers → enquiry.
- Removed the abstract introductory manifesto, repeated interactive bottle study and six-step process. The real product case demonstrates the method directly.
- Reduced decorative captions, repeated claims, gallery metadata and pricing prose. Kept the static-form disclosure because visitors need to understand what submission actually does.
- Larger, quieter editorial layout with one photographic campaign image, tactile metal, orange and architectural light. Concept attribution stays as a small caption.
- Desktop image movement is limited to a slow translation within its frame. Work and result images reveal on entry; research keeps the product alongside the insights. No empty pinned scrolling. Mobile gives the headline, offer and action priority before the full uncropped image. Reduced motion removes hero movement and sticky positioning.
- The original ScrollCraft engine remains unchanged. This is a simplification of the existing website, not a new fingerprint or a new independent site build.

## Image asset

Generated with the built-in Imagegen tool. Final assets: `public/assets/hero-editorial.webp` and `public/assets/hero-editorial-small.webp`. Source remains in the generator output folder. Both served variants preserve the generated composition.

### Generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: NONOBJECT design studio website hero, a premium editorial product still life. Create a photorealistic high-end campaign photograph in a vertical 4:5 composition. One ordinary, unbranded vivid vermilion-orange satin aluminum cylindrical refill bottle with a simple flush screw cap, no pump, elevated by exceptional art direction. Bottle is balanced diagonally on the crisp crease of a single wide sculptural sheet of brushed polished aluminum, which bends once into a sweeping asymmetrical arc; physically plausible contact and weight. The bottle is the unmistakable focal subject, large occupying about 60% of image height, close-up with beautiful fine surface grain. Quiet warm ivory studio backdrop and floor, architectural sunlight entering from upper left, deep elegant soft-edged shadow to lower right, sophisticated silver reflections of orange, nuanced dark metal edges. Shot as luxury industrial design editorial, medium format lens, tactile sharp material, understated and confident, not playful CGI. Composition has asymmetric tension, bottle leaning slightly left, arc framing its bottom right without obscuring it, all main elements fully inside image with generous 8% breathing room. Palette exclusively vermilion orange, silver, warm cream and deep shadow. No text, no logos, no typography, no labels, no floating pieces, no extra props, no icons, no gradients as graphic effects, no transparent background. This is a conceptual studio visual, not a client product.

## Validation

The interaction test verifies gallery use and the shortened enquiry, including 100+ products without files, retry, download and mobile layout. The visual harness samples six viewports including 320px and reduced motion, checks overflow, image loading, runtime errors and automated accessibility, and captures hero scroll positions, selected work, research and pricing. Actual phone hardware and real form delivery are not covered; Pages remains a clearly labelled static preview.
