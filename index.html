A few details.

What do you need to get started?+

A short brief with your product count, platforms and timeline. Photos or a shared folder are welcome, but optional. We’ll collect SKU details and specifications after we get in touch.

How long does it take?+

Usually up to 5 working days, depending on scope and availability. We confirm timing before work begins. Rush work is available by agreement with a provisional 30% surcharge.

How do payment and revisions work?+

50% to begin, 50% after approval. Two consolidated revision rounds are included. Additional rounds or a change of concept are quoted separately. For 10 or more images per product, we prepare a custom quote.

Where can I use the visuals?+

Amazon, Allegro, eBay, Shopify and other online stores. We agree on the platforms and check their requirements before designing your set.

How will I receive the files?+

You review watermarked previews first. After full payment, original files are delivered through a shared folder, organised by product. Files remain available for one month; longer retention is agreed separately.
